## E1: can Bryan reach owners with recurring pain?

- **Audience:** developers who own a private HTTPS app or local MCP service and have a real caller outside that environment. Seek human-sharing and Claude.ai MCP cases separately; no assumed pool of prospects.
- **Exact task:** obtain a written account of the last outside-caller task, when it happened, occurrences in the last month, workaround, and whether that workaround already solves it. The team stimulus asks whether a second maintainer actually shares the service; liking a team page does not qualify anyone.
- **Baseline:** the participant's actual last workaround, including an existing access product, moving the agent onto private compute, or not exposing the service. Record it verbatim; no unsupported superiority claims.
- **Denominators:** all approved invitations, all responses, all qualifying written responses and recipe counts. Keep no-response, refusal, dropout and exclusion records. Missing invitation counts remain undefined. Never replace an inconvenient respondent.
- **Success:** by day 14, ten qualifying written responses; at least six describe at least two occurrences in the last month, including at least three browser-sharing and three MCP cases. Use a preregistered ten-person response cohort; extras require another protocol and cannot rescue this one.
- **Kill:** fewer than ten qualifying responses or fewer than six recurring cases (or either recipe below three) when day 14 or 50 recruitment/approval minutes is reached. Narrow to a demonstrated segment or stop the two-recipe commercial test. An open, unfinished window stays pending.
- **Owner-time ceiling:** 50 recruitment/approval minutes across weeks 1–2, inside the thesis's 120 minutes each week. Record response review and support separately; the ceiling is not reset by exclusions.
- **Required real evidence:** independent written responses with local receipt references, last occurrence, workaround, actual outside-caller ownership, invitation ledger and elapsed-day/owner-time ledger. Simulated respondents never satisfy E1.

## E2: does the whole task beat the strongest viable alternative?

- **Audience:** six novices, three per recipe, selected from the same pain cohort where practical. Preregister participant/task identities and recipe assignment before attempts; include missing attempts and dropouts in the six.
- **Exact MCP task:** connect the disposable **Cinder Almanac** read-only service to a supported Claude.ai connector, call `read_almanac`, obtain `orbital-garden: green`, deny an ungranted caller, then revoke the original grant and verify denial. The local walkthrough only imitates those steps.
- **Exact browser task:** share the disposable **Mosslight Review** app with one intended email; retrieve `layout-check: ready`, deny a different email, revoke access and verify denial. Ask about shared service ownership only after completing the task.
- **Baseline:** first reproduce the same dummy tasks on tunn, Pomerium `pom.run`, Cloudflare Access/MCP and Pangolin wherever currently supported. Preserve current primary documentation, tested versions, account prerequisites and receipts. Each novice compares tunn to their strongest viable alternative. Record unsupported cases with a current receipt; absence of a receipt is unknown. Never handicap a substitute or insert fake waiting time.
- **Order:** alternate tunn-first and baseline-first within each recipe (two/one, reversed for the other recipe). Measure all active setup work from the install page, including login, connector configuration, consent and active handling of email wait. Record elapsed wall time and passive wait separately. Do not advertise a 60-second result: ask **“Can you complete this in 60 seconds?”** and measure.
- **Success:** at least four of the original six complete the useful task in at most 300 active seconds, at most half their own alternative's total active setup time, and choose tunn for the next real task. Report each recipe separately even though the frozen E2 threshold is pooled. A page click or fixture result is not useful real completion.
- **Kill:** fewer than four qualifying comparative successes after the six-person window closes. If an incumbent ties and users prefer it, stop a generic clone and retain only a specific observed gap. Any unsafe grant stops hosted testing regardless of timing. Safety and real interoperability are separate prerequisites, never inferred from this kit.
- **Owner-time ceiling:** proposed 35 minutes for Bryan's asynchronous comparative evidence review in the days 15–21 slot, within the thesis's 120-minute weekly ceiling. Participant time is separate. Freeze closure at day 21; missing evidence cannot become a pass when the time budget expires. Missing substitute/security prerequisites block the trial and are reported explicitly.
- **Required real evidence:** counterbalanced attempt order, strongest-viable baseline reason, baseline and tunn task receipts, measured timing and assistance, useful result, deny/revoke receipts, safety review and supported-client receipt, next-real-task choice. No mocks count toward E2.

## Collection rules

Use pseudonymous IDs and clone-local private receipts; do not commit actual customer records. Keep fixed rosters, failures, churn, nulls and original attempts. Record support minutes separately from development/review. Record voluntary repeat tasks with dates and exclude health checks, retries, reminders and forced exercises. Record price offered, response, written intent and collected payment independently. No simulated observation, duplicate identity, duplicate task, missing receipt, incomplete attempt or unknown denominator can create a real PASS. An actual label is a collector assertion, not independently verified evidence; Bryan must review receipts before making a business decision.

Changing a target, cohort or offer requires a new explicit protocol before new observations; never tune this version after looking at results. No real-host work may begin without E4's independent security review and current client compatibility acceptance. No OAuth or SSH implementation is assigned to this experiment.
