Trust and availability

The relay terminates TLS and can read HTTP traffic. Private tunnels check access at the relay; this is not end-to-end encryption. You can self-host to control the relay.

Your local app and tunn publisher must stay running and connected. Relay availability alone does not mean your app is reachable. There is no uptime guarantee.
