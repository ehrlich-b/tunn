Quickstart · macOS · Linux

Give port 3000 a URL.

Install, sign in once, then run one command next to your app. tunn and your app must keep running.

01

Install and sign in

curl -fsSL https://tunn.to/install.sh | sh
tunn login

Intel or ARM. The downloader verifies the latest release from https://tunn.to/dl; manual downloads are on GitLab releases. If tunn is not found, add $HOME/.local/bin to PATH. For source builds and recovery, see the install guide.

02

Share port 3000

Have your local app running on port 3000. Replace the email below with the person you want to invite.

tunn 3000 --allow=alex@example.com

tunn prints the HTTPS URL. Keep tunn running in this terminal, alongside your app. Send the link to your invited person; they sign in with GitHub or an email link. Everyone else gets a 403 at the edge, before anything reaches your app.

Other ways to share

Choose one of these alternatives when you need a different audience:

# only you
tunn 3000
# everyone at a domain
tunn 3000 --allow=@example.com
# anyone with the link
tunn 3000 --public

For public webhooks, verify signatures at the edge and keep your app's own check on.

03

Watch requests and usage

Optional: restart tunn with inspection enabled. Press Ctrl-C in the terminal running the previous tunn process, and keep your local app running. Reuse the invited person's email below.

tunn 3000 --allow=alex@example.com --inspect

Use the newly printed HTTPS URL, and keep this tunn process running.

In another terminal:

tunn usage

The local inspector stores headers, not bodies, unless you add --inspect-body. Your account also has a request inspector. Quotas cover all your endpoints and reset monthly in UTC. At a limit, tunn stops; there are no overage charges. Plans and units

04

Connect a local MCP server

tunn mcp -- ./my-mcp-server

MCP endpoints are private by default. Copy the printed /mcp URL into your client, sign in and approve access. Full walkthrough

05

Coming from ngrok

# ngrok http 3000
tunn 3000 --public
# ngrok http 3000 --subdomain=work
tunn 3000 --name=work --public

Run tunn login instead of setting an authtoken. ngrok domains, config files and traffic policies do not carry over. Free keeps one name, Pro keeps ten, random names included. tunn vs ngrok
