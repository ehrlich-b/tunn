tunn is a command-line publisher and a relay. It forwards visits to an HTTPS URL to an app running on your machine.

How it works

The publisher keeps an outbound connection to the relay. A visitor connects to the relay, which forwards the request to your local app. Keep both your app and the publisher running and connected.

TLS ends at the tunn relay, which can read traffic. New tunnels are private to their owner. Allow other verified email addresses or choose public access.

Are you an agent?

Choose who can open it.

Only you can open a new tunnel. Add someone’s email to share it.

tunn 3000 --allow=alex@example.com,sam@example.com

Alex and Sam sign in to personal accounts with the listed emails. Browsers without a session go to sign-in; signed-in people outside the allow list are denied before a request reaches your app. Manage or revoke access in Share access.

Public webhooks: tunn 3000 --public lets anyone with the URL connect. Verify signatures in your app.
