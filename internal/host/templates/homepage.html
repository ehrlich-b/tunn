Are you an agent?

Choose who can open it.

Only you can open a new tunnel. Add someone’s email to share it.

tunn 3000 --allow=alex@example.com,sam@example.com

Alex and Sam sign in to personal accounts with the listed emails using Google or an email link where offered. Manage people or revoke access in Share access.

Webhooks need a separate choice. Tunnels start private; a webhook sender cannot use browser sign-in. Use tunn 3000 --public for callbacks and verify the provider’s signature in your app. Anyone with that URL can reach it.

Keep your app’s own security. For OpenClaw, configure the allowed origin, token and pairing. Test live updates on your phone before relying on it away from home. WebSockets are supported; mobile and OpenClaw compatibility still need manual checks.

Know the trust boundary. TLS terminates at the tunn relay. The relay can read traffic. Authentication and access details.
