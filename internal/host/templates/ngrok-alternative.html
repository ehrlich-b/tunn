An ngrok alternative for local MCP and webhooks

tunn gives your local server an HTTPS URL, with private MCP OAuth, email sharing and account quotas. Choose it when you want local tools and a fixed subscription bill.

Compare the actual units

tunn's transfer quota includes HTTP bodies in both directions, WebSocket wire frames and TCP payloads, excluding transport framing. Admissions include MCP/HTTP calls and connection opens. ngrok counts outbound transfer and HTTP requests. These figures are not a workload benchmark.

Choose the tool that fits

ngrok's broader gateway, policy, compliance and enterprise offerings may fit your requirements better. Its free endpoints have no session timeout and already include a stable dev domain. tunn has no published enterprise SLA, and local MCP connector acceptance must be verified in your client. Your laptop and server must remain online.

Other options deserve a look: Cloudflare Quick Tunnels are free but have a 200 in-flight limit and no SSE; named Cloudflare Tunnel is a separate setup. Pinggy Pro advertises $3/month or $30/year per seat, cheaper than tunn. These sources were fetched 2026-10-11.
