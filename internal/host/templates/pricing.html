What counts toward a limit

All endpoints and callers share your account's budgets, which reset on the first day of each UTC month. Each HTTP or MCP request counts once, and so does each WebSocket or TCP connection. Transfer sums request and response bodies, WebSocket frames and TCP payloads in both directions, excluding TLS, HTTP and relay framing.

At a quota, tunn stops admitting requests or moving bytes and reports the reason. There are no automatic overage charges. Check usage in your account or with tunn usage. Upgrading raises the allowance; it does not reset this month's usage. A stream can be cut off after its response headers have started.

Uploads are limited to 100 MiB. Per-tunnel bandwidth ceilings apply as abuse guards. Your local server must keep running.
