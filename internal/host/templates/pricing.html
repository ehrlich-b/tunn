What counts toward a limit?

All endpoints and callers share your account's budgets. They reset on the first day of each UTC calendar month. HTTP and MCP admissions count once per request; each WebSocket or TCP connection counts once. Transfer sums HTTP request/response bodies, WebSocket wire frames and TCP payloads in both directions. TLS, HTTP and relay framing are excluded.

At a quota, tunn stops admitting requests or transferring bytes and reports a limit reason. There are no automatic overage charges. Usage is shown in your account and with tunn usage. Upgrading raises the allowance; it does not reset this month's usage. Stream cutoffs can occur after response headers have started.

Uploads are limited to 100 MiB. Per-tunnel bandwidth ceilings apply as abuse guards. Stream and application limits still apply. Your local server must keep running.

Keep a connector or webhook URL

Free automatically attempts to reserve {{.FreeReservations}} name as you publish; Pro attempts up to {{.Reservations}}. Choose names with --subdomain before publishing; random names also use reservation slots. At the reservation limit, additional names are temporary. Unreserved names can be taken by someone else while offline. For reservation changes, contact support. See the quickstart.
